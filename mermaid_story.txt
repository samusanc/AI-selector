Coral lived in a shimmering coral kingdom, but unlike her sisters, she had legs instead of a graceful tail. She watched wistfully as they danced through the currents, feeling like an outcast. The ocean, her home, felt vast and unnavigable without fins. She often sat on a smooth rock, gazing at the surface, dreaming of a world where she belonged.

One day, an ancient, barnacle-covered sea turtle named Old Man Tiber approached her. "Your legs are not a curse, little one," he rumbled. "They are a bridge." He led her to a hidden cove where the water was shallow enough to walk, revealing vibrant tide pools and sun-drenched beaches. Coral discovered the joy of exploring both land and sea, her unique form allowing her adventures no other mermaid could dream of. She became the most curious and widely traveled mermaid, her legs her greatest asset.
