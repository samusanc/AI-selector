Elara was no ordinary elephant. While her herd thundered across the savanna, leaving craters with their massive feet, Elara's steps were surprisingly delicate. Her footprints, barely larger than a gazelle's, were a constant source of wonder and sometimes, gentle teasing. The other elephants would joke, "Did a mouse walk here, Elara?" But Elara didn't mind. Her small prints allowed her to navigate treacherous rocky paths and dense undergrowth with an agility none of her kin possessed. One dry season, when the usual watering holes vanished, it was Elara, with her nimble feet, who discovered a hidden spring deep within a thorny thicket, saving her entire herd from thirst. Her small prints, once a peculiarity, became a symbol of unique strength.
